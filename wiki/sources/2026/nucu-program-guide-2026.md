# NUCU Program Guide — 2026

The archived **NUCU 2026 Program Guide** describes a University of Colorado Boulder × Nagoya University program titled “Entrepreneurship and Innovation in Aging Societies,” planned for **May 14–28, 2026: 15 days / 14 nights**. Tokyo and Nagoya are the main locations, with urban and rural comparisons including Obu and Nakatsugawa. This is a record of the guide's plans and claims, not proof that events occurred or a current 2027 itinerary.

## Source and program structure

The sole new source is the cleaned text export identifying its original document as `NUCU2026_Program_Guide.pdf`. Scope is determined by its `2026/` directory. No publication date is inferred from the travel dates. The provisional party was **20 students, one University of Colorado chaperone, and one CoHo staff member**.

The guide divides the program into May 14–17 arrival/Tokyo startup visits/culture/transfer; May 18–22 Nagoya fieldwork and site visits; and May 23–28 presentations, closing activities, optional culture, Tokyo return, National Diet, alumni reception, reflection, and departure. CU-only activities are distinguished from CU & NU activities; Nagoya University students were invited to marked activities May 18–23. Faculty-only events must not be interpreted as student invitations.

Objectives include understanding aging-related demographic, healthcare, mobility and community challenges; studying hospitals, startups, government initiatives and university research; applying entrepreneurial/design-thinking approaches through interviews, surveys, group research and presentations; comparing urban/rural Japan; and developing cross-cultural collaboration with faculty, practitioners, entrepreneurs, residents and peers. Hospital surveys concern user needs and patient/family experiences. Teams were to synthesize fieldwork and stakeholder evidence at NIC, with faculty and stakeholder feedback.
