# Ludus Labs Website Snapshot — 2026-08-24

The Ludus Labs company website snapshot captured on **2026-08-24** describes a startup incubator using emerging technologies and international university teams to address healthcare, demographic change and education. It promotes NUCU 2026, learning through building, and an international alumni community. This page preserves the archived 2026 snapshot; its benefits and application labels do not establish current availability or 2027 arrangements.

## Source and organization

The local text identifies its source as **https://www.luduslabs.co**, with a capture date of **2026-08-24** and footer **© 2026 Ludus Labs**. The capture date is not necessarily the site's publication date. The page uses “Build Globally” and “Building global impact through innovation.” Its account credits sponsors, university partners, volunteers and mentors with making the work possible; it names no individual sponsor or mentor.

Ludus Labs describes itself as a startup incubator creating impact through emerging technologies with global reach. Cross-university teams from around the world work on healthcare, demographic change and education. Its founders are described collectively as educators, designers, entrepreneurs and researchers who believe the best way to learn is to build. Students are offered tools, space and “a bit of chaos” to work on urgent challenges; no founders are individually identified.

The work began in Japan, while the community is described as global. NUCU connects **Nagoya University (NU)** and the **University of Colorado (CU)**, bringing students across countries and disciplines into startup teams through both online and in-person formats. The website says **Ludus** means both **play and school in Latin**, expressing serious learning through playful experimentation. The stated role is to help students learn how to build rather than prescribe what they should build. Its aims include collaboration, creativity and courage beyond a single program.

## NUCU 2026 promotion

The listing is titled **“NUCU 2026 - Build in Japan: AI & Automation for Tomorrow's Societies”** and names **Nagoya University & University of Colorado, Boulder**. Its description, **“Study Abroad Meets a Startup Studio,”** combines global entrepreneurship, AI-driven automation and cross-cultural collaboration to address what it calls population collapse. Activities are a **design sprint**, **test market research** and an **international pitch competition**, with hands-on technology innovation and startup development in an intercultural setting.

The five promotional points are **15 days in Japan**, **Build an Idea with Impact**, **Earn Academic Credit & Microcredentials**, **Global Networking**, and **More Than a Class**. This snapshot specifies no credit quantity, credential provider, award conditions, price or trip dates. See [[nucu-program-2026]] for separately attributed course and itinerary information.

## Intended participants

The website's framing is “The old safety nets are gone, but so are the barriers.” It identifies four audiences:

- **Purpose Driven Builders:** students testing bold ideas, launching meaningful ventures and shaping their futures through creation rather than consumption.
- **Creative Technologists:** people using emerging technology to build immersive, human-centered experiences that change how people live and connect.
- **Systems Hackers:** changemakers experimenting with difficult, complex problems.
- **Global Team Players:** collaborators across cultures and time zones developing communication, leadership and innovation in diverse real-world teams.

These descriptions are intended audiences, not formal admissions criteria.

## Six advertised learning benefits

1. **Cross-Cultural Innovation:** teamwork and communication across cultures.
2. **Business Validation:** validate business models and frame solutions to pressing challenges.
3. **Applied AI Learning:** work on an impact startup team and develop knowledge of AI and other emerging technologies.
4. **Network for the Future:** connections with global innovators, entrepreneurs and business professionals.
5. **Certify Skills, Earn Credit:** a digital certificate and academic credit **at eligible schools**.
6. **Stay Connected, Give Back:** **lifetime access** to an alumni community of global peers, with the **chance** to return as an ambassador helping lead future programs.

These are website promises, not evidence of measured outcomes, guaranteed credits at every institution or a guaranteed ambassador appointment. Example impact areas are healthcare for longer-lived societies, demographic change as an opportunity for innovation, and the future of learning. See [[nucu-startup-learning-2026]].
