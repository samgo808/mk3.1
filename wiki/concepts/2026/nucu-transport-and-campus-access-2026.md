# NUCU Transport and Campus Access — 2026

The archived 2026 NUCU guide plans **Shinkansen transfers between Tokyo and Nagoya**, local train/subway/bus travel and charter buses for field visits. It tells participants to keep an **IC card or sufficient cash** for uncovered transport/meals and reconfirm meeting points, departure times and routes each evening. Its bus instructions conflict internally, and maps are incomplete in the text export, so this page records evidence rather than supplies verified current navigation.

## Conflicting information — Nagoya bus procedure (pages 27–28)

The guide tells riders to locate a marked bus stop, check its route/timetable, bus number, direction and arrival time; prepare small change or an IC card such as **Manaca or Toica**; and check the arriving vehicle's destination. It says to **board through the front door**, tap a card beside the driver when boarding, or take a numbered ticket if applicable when paying cash.

It describes a **flat adult fare of ¥210**, with **Late Night Buses excepted**, yet also instructs cash passengers to check a fare chart against a numbered ticket at alighting and insert the ticket before cash. It says card users should tap again when alighting. For getting off, it first says go to the **front** to exit and later says exit through the **back**.

These mixed flat/distance-fare, payment/tap and exit instructions are unresolved. Do not silently choose one procedure or present the compiled account as reliable bus-use directions. The source also advises holding handrails/seating safely, monitoring announcements/displays and pressing the stop button before the destination.

**Review decision, 2026-09-28:** the reviewer instructed “keep source discrepancy for bus instructions.” The discrepancy and caveat are retained as written. This editorial decision does not verify a bus procedure. Page-level public-release approval was subsequently granted on 2026-09-28.

## Higashiyama and NIC (pages 33–34)

The **National Innovation Complex (NIC)** has **Idea Stoa on 1F** and a **3F conference room**. The **parking area in front of Toyoda Auditorium** is the assembly location for charter trips. The export contains a “Dormitory to NIC” caption but no usable route image; no further turns are inferred.

## Tsurumai access (pages 35–38)

The guide's prose route to the **Basic Medical Research Building / Basic Medicine Building** meeting rooms is: **Tsurumai subway Exit 4 or JR Meidaibyoinmae Exit → along the JR line with Tsurumai Park on the right → School of Medicine West Gate** (described as between a building under construction and a nurses' residence) **→ Basic Medical Research Building → meeting room**. It identifies a **first-floor meeting room**, Meeting Rooms **No. 1 and No. 2**, and the **Meidaibyoin / Meidai Byoin bus stop**.

Separate image labels read A subway Exit 4; B along JR line; C JR Meidaibyoin Exit; D Tsurumai Campus; E pass hospital front gate; F enter campus; G turn left at Basic Medical Research Building; H enter building. The text alone does not establish whether these labels depict the same gate route as the prose.

Captions include dormitory-to-Tsurumai, bus-stop-to-Basic-Medicine-Building and **JR Nagoya Station to Tsurumai Station**. Hospital-map labels include main entrance, outpatient building, ward building, consultation buildings A/B, research buildings 1/2/3, Basic Research Building, JR Tsurumai Station and Tsurumai Park. Their spatial relationships are not recoverable from the text.
