# NUCU Nagoya Schedule, May 21–23 — 2026

The archived 2026 NUCU guide plans rural fieldwork on May 21, hospital surveys and preparation on May 22, and final presentations on May 23. These are joint CU & NU days; residence walks are specified for CU participants. Source pages 3–4 and 9–10 contain provisional plans. Human review on 2026-09-28 resolved the May 23 timing: presentations start at **13:00**, followed by sushi from **15:00 to 16:00**.

## Thursday May 21 — Nakatsugawa, Iwamura and elderly care

- **07:30:** CU students assemble at Yamate South entrance with Samuel; **07:35** walk to Higashiyama Campus/Toyoda Auditorium.
- **07:50:** all-student assembly in front of Toyoda Auditorium; **08:00** charter-bus departure for Nakatsugawa.
- **09:45:** arrival/short break; **10:00** Dr. Ban lecture at **Agi Clinic**; **10:45** facility tour.
- **11:30:** charter transfer from Agi area to Iwamura; **11:45** walk around **Iwamura Castle Town** and lunch.
- **13:45:** assemble and move to **Cyclamen Elder Care Center**, also called **Cyclamen Elderly care house** in the route column.
- **14:00:** elderly-care tour and interviews; **15:30** close; **15:40** charter departure toward Nagoya/Higashiyama Campus.
- **17:15:** campus arrival; **17:20** CU students walk back to Yamate South. The overview also lists team preparation.

The rural-tour prose calls Nakatsugawa about an hour from Nagoya, while this itinerary allocates 1 hour 45 minutes outbound. Preserve both as an approximate prose description and a specific planned route duration; neither is a current travel-time guarantee.

## Friday May 22 — survey and presentation preparation

CU route: **08:10** assemble at Yamate South entrance with Samuel; **08:20** walk to Yagoto Nisseki; **08:40** subway to Tsurumai, transferring if needed; **09:05** station arrival; **09:10** campus walk.

All students: **09:20** assemble at **Meeting Room No. 1, Basic Medicine Building**; **09:30** information session; **10:00** survey at Nagoya University Hospital; **11:30** survey wrap-up/data organization in Room No. 1; **12:00** lunch at campus/cafe; **13:00** group discussion and organization of interview/survey findings. **Room No. 1 is available until 19:00**. Break, team preparation and CU return to residence are untimed.

The guide frames surveys/interviews as a way to understand users, patients and families and connect observed needs to entrepreneurial solutions. It does not contain individual survey responses or authorize publishing patient information.

## Saturday May 23 — final presentations and closing activities

**09:40** CU residence-entrance assembly; **09:45** walk to NIC; **10:00** all-student assembly at NIC; **10:10** group preparation in **NIC Conference Room**; **12:00** lunch/final preparation; **12:45** presentation setup at **NIC / Idea Stoa**; **13:00** presentations with students, faculty and guests.

## Conflicting information — resolved by human review

The reviewer confirmed on 2026-09-28: **presentations start at 13:00, followed by the sushi party from 15:00 to 16:00**. Use these times for the archived 2026 itinerary; no separate presentation end time is inferred. The sushi party has **no additional fee** in both source descriptions.

For source traceability, the raw overview gives presentations **13:00–14:30** and sushi **14:30–16:00**, while the detailed schedule gives presentations starting **13:00**, sushi starting **15:00** and ending **16:00**. The reviewer's decision resolves this discrepancy without modifying the raw source.

CU students return to residence afterward. An additional evening closing dinner/gathering remains **optional, venue TBD**, rather than a confirmed reservation. Yamate South is the planned lodging for all three nights.
