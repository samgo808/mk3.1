# NUCU Nagoya Schedule, May 18–20 — 2026

The archived NUCU 2026 guide plans joint CU & NU learning activities in Nagoya on May 18–20, with separate CU residence transfers and faculty-only appointments. The times below consolidate pages 3 and 6–9 into chronological routes, without filling blank times. This is a provisional 2026 schedule, not current travel advice or proof of completed visits.

## Monday May 18 — orientation, hospital and xR Center

CU route: **08:30** Best Western Plus checkout/lobby assembly; **08:40** depart by train/bus for Yamate South; **10:00** residence check-in with Samuel and Hasegawa; **11:00** room setup/free time; **12:10** entrance assembly with Samuel; **12:20** walk to Yagoto Nisseki Station; **12:40** subway to Tsurumai; **13:05** arrive; **13:10** walk to Tsurumai Campus.

All students: **13:20** assemble in **Meeting Room No. 1, Basic Medicine Building**; **13:30** orientation; **14:15** Nagoya University Hospital tour with **Dr Sumigama–OBGY**; **15:30** xR Center/medical innovation with **Dr Nakanishi–GI and Ms. Sato**; **16:30** close/break.

CU return route: **16:40** leave Basic Medicine Building for Tsurumai Station; **16:55** subway to Yagoto Nisseki; **17:20** walk to residence; **17:30** arrive at Yamate South.

Faculty route: **16:50** Prof. Matt, Samuel and Hasegawa move to Dean's Room; **17:00–17:30** visit Dean Katsuno; **18:40** depart campus/hotel for **Umenohana Sakae**; **19:00–21:00** official welcome dinner. The detailed dinner list is Prof. Matt, Samuel, Prof. Kasuya, Hasegawa and invited faculty. The day's CU & NU label must not be read as a student invitation to faculty appointments/dinner. Public release of these archived staff-only arrangements was approved on 2026-09-28.

## Tuesday May 19 — regional entrepreneurship and clinical innovation

CU route with Samuel: **08:40** Yamate South entrance assembly; **08:50** walk to Yagoto Nisseki; **09:10** subway toward Tsurumai; **09:35** station arrival; **09:40** walk to STATION Ai.

All students: **09:50** STATION Ai assembly/preparation; **10:00** Aichi Prefecture briefing and **10:30** Tongali/Nagoya University entrepreneurship overview in **3F Central Lounge**; **11:00** break; **11:30** STATION Ai tour; **12:00** lunch at STATION Ai/Tsurumai area; **12:45** transfer to Tsurumai Campus/Medical Department; **13:00** arrival.

**Meeting Room No. 2, Basic Medicine Building:** **13:10** Professor Masakatsu Fukai, Medical Innovation and Industry-Academia Collaboration; **14:10** session closes/break; **14:30** Dr. Oyama; **15:30** PREVENT session by Mr. Hagiwara; **16:30** close/Q&A. Team preparation in Room No. 2 and CU return to Yamate South follow, with no times specified.

## Wednesday May 20 — NCGG, robotics and mobility

**08:00** CU assembly at Yamate South with Samuel; **08:05** walk to Higashiyama Campus/Toyoda Auditorium; **08:20** all-student assembly in front of Toyoda Auditorium; **08:30** charter bus to **National Center for Geriatrics and Gerontology (NCGG)**; **09:20** arrival/check-in; **09:30** introduction/information session; **10:30** robotics center tour; **11:30** visit closes; **11:40** depart for **Agri Town Genki**; **12:00** lunch.

**12:40** charter transfer back toward Higashiyama Campus; **13:30** NIC arrival; **14:00** Professor Hirofumi Aoki driving-simulation session at NIC/driving-simulation facility; **15:30** close. Group discussion/team preparation and CU return to Yamate South are untimed. Additional university research is included in the overview only **if arranged**. Optional NU/CU karaoke or bowling has venue/time TBD.

All three nights are planned at Yamate South. The source labels charter travel on May 20 and primarily train/bus movement on May 18–19. It instructs participants to reconfirm the next day's route and timing rather than rely on an outdated schedule.
