# NUCU Tokyo Itinerary and Transfers — 2026

The archived 2026 NUCU guide plans CU-only Tokyo activities on May 14–17 and May 25–28, plus a CU-only optional Nagoya culture day on May 24. These are source schedules with unspecified times and locations, not confirmed attendance records or 2027 arrangements. Sources: overview pages 3–4 and detailed itinerary pages 5–6, 11–12.

## Initial Tokyo stay and Nagoya transfer

| Day/date | Planned sequence |
|---|---|
| 1, Thursday May 14 | Narita arrival **16:00** in the overview; immigration and baggage claim; assemble after baggage claim at a meeting point TBD; prepare train tickets/IC cards; train to Tokyo and check into **KOKO Ginza Tsukiji**; free time and confirm the next day's meeting time/route. |
| 2, Friday May 15 | Assemble at hotel lobby; local transit to **Google for Startups** (specific venue TBD); check in; **Sunshine Burri, 10:30–12:00**; lunch/transfer and prepare for **Techstars Tokyo**, specific venue TBD; **Rene Budeck, 14:00–15:00**; Q&A/wrap-up; return or small-group exploration; KOKO overnight and prepare for culture day. |
| 3, Saturday May 16 | Hotel-lobby assembly; train to Asakusa; **Sanja Matsuri**, local lunch, continued cultural exploration; train/bus back or free time; KOKO overnight; pack and confirm the Shinkansen plan. |
| 4, Sunday May 17 | Hotel checkout/lobby assembly; train to Tokyo Station; prepare food/drink; Shinkansen to Nagoya Station; lunch/local transfer; check into **Best Western Plus**; rest/local orientation/free time; confirm Yamate South check-in for May 18. |

Tokyo startup visits introduce entrepreneurship, investment, mentorship and scaling in Japan and internationally. The guide provides session times in the overview even where the detailed table leaves them blank. Times for other steps are not inferred.

## Lodging and uncertainty

The initial Tokyo hotel, the May 17 Best Western Plus stay, May 18–24 Yamate South nights and return Tokyo hotel are all archived plans. Page 1's abbreviated lodging list omits the intermediate Best Western Plus night that the itinerary explicitly includes. No flight number or full timed airport transfer is supplied. Intercity travel uses the Shinkansen; students were instructed to reconfirm meeting points, departure times and transport each evening.
