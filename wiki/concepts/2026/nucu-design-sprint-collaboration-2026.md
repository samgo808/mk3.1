# NUCU Design Sprint and International Team Decisions — 2026

The archived March–April 2026 NUCU material connects a virtual design sprint with international teamwork, field learning and venture development. Teams are asked to create a **tech-enabled solution that helps society adapt to demographic change**, not to solve population decline itself. The evidence consists of a short March outline and later debriefs, not complete recordings of every sprint activity.

## Outline and missing mapping

The outline names sessions **8–11**, dated **March 3, 10, 24 and 31, 2026**. It lists five day labels:

1. Program overview, problem context, **MonsterWorkshop**.
2. **Sailboat**, **HMW**.
3. Optional **special lecture**.
4. **Lightening/Lightning Demos**, **Crazy 8s**, solution sketches.
5. **Art Museum**, straw poll + supervote, storyboard.

There is no mapping from five days to four dates, no fifth date and no detailed exercise instructions. HMW is an undefined acronym in this source. See [[2026-03-nucu-sessions-8-11]].

## What the April debrief adds

On **April 7**, the instructor reports that the sprint ran out of time before selecting a direction. Nagoya teammates had finished their virtual program and resumed classes. The task is to reconnect through Discord or an existing mutually used channel and obtain input asynchronously rather than assume silence equals agreement.

In **Miro**, green sticky dots produce a heat map of interest in solution sketches. The example is about **20 dots**, with **five** on one sketch and **10** on another. A **red star / supervote** selects a direction. Exact allocation and tie rules are not supplied. Teams may request missing sketches and should still make a useful choice if some people do not respond; the lecture encourages outreach and consensus, while allowing a later pivot in Japan.

On **April 14**, voting and a site-preference survey are requested by **Friday** (the calendar would suggest April 17, but the transcript says only Friday). The site survey solicits interests in companies and areas such as drones, healthcare and AI; visits remain subject to availability and logistics. These are archived deadlines and requests.

## Connect the idea to field evidence

The April 7 video depicts the previous year's hospital interviews, xR and autonomous-vehicle labs, STATION Ai, rural transport/healthcare, a mayor's meeting, a drone startup and final pitches at Idea Stoa. It is background for planning, not a confirmed 2026 schedule. Teams should identify the affected user, learn the constraints and test assumptions before refining a pitch.

The April 14 technical session adds rapid prototypes using Glide, Lovable or coding assistants. A functioning-looking screen is not proof of user demand or valid data. The instructor warns that automating a bad idea quickly still produces a bad idea. See [[nucu-ai-assisted-prototyping-2026]], [[2026-04-07-nucu-session-12]] and [[2026-04-14-nucu-session-13]].
