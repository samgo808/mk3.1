# Nagoya University Background in the NUCU Guide — 2026

The archived 2026 NUCU guide provides background on **Nagoya University, its medical schools, NIC and Toyoda Auditorium**. This page preserves that document's institutional and architectural assertions as source claims; it does not verify current appointments, building history or heritage status. Source pages 29–30 and 33–34 support the account.

## University and medical education

The guide traces Nagoya University's medical-school heritage to a **provisional hospital and medical school for the Nagoya Domain in 1871**. It describes a research university associated with multiple Nobel laureates and a goal of becoming a major Asian medical education/research hub through global partnerships.

Three named educational programs illustrate its approach:

- **NOVI+A:** community-oriented medical education, near-peer teaching and onsite, virtual and anthropological learning.
- **CIBoG:** informatics combined with biomedical science to address global health challenges.
- **AI-MAILs:** academia–industry links to develop leaders in medical AI.

The guide says international exchanges resumed after COVID-19 and that Nagoya hosted the **6th annual GAME conference in November 2022**. It describes GAME as a strategic consortium of medical schools supporting medical education and collaborative international research. The guide does not expand every program acronym or identify all partner institutions, so no expansions or roster are invented.

## Named leadership in the guide

**Masahisa Katsuno** is listed as Professor and Dean of Nagoya University School of Medicine and Graduate School of Medicine, with **Department of Virology** printed beneath the entry. **Hideki Kasuya** is listed as Professor, Vice Dean for International Affairs, Department of International Education, and Chair of the Office of International Affairs. These are reproduced as source attributions; the departmental assignment and roles have not been independently checked.

The medical school/hospital institutional address is **65, Tsurumai-cho, Showa-ku, Nagoya, Aichi 466-8550, Japan**. Staff contact details are recorded in the source page; the human reviewer approved their public release on 2026-09-28.

## National Innovation Complex (NIC)

**NIC** means **National Innovation Complex**, at Higashiyama Campus. The guide identifies **Idea Stoa on the first floor** as a co-creation space for students, researchers, entrepreneurs and other collaborators, and a **conference room on the third floor**. NIC hosts planned driving simulation, team preparation and final presentations; Idea Stoa is named for the presentations and sushi gathering.

## Toyoda Auditorium — unverified source assertions

The guide describes **Toyoda Auditorium** at Higashiyama Campus as established in **1960**, funded by a **Toyota Motor Corporation** donation, designed by **Fumihiko Maki**, an example of **Brutalist architecture**, and registered as a **National Tangible Cultural Property of Japan in 2011**. These specific architectural/historical assertions require verification before being used as established facts; this ingestion preserves rather than silently corrects them.

It describes concrete/geometric forms, functionality/acoustics and use for commencements, academic conferences, lectures and cultural performances. The **parking space in front of the auditorium** is the guide's assembly area. Missing map imagery prevents inferring additional directions.
