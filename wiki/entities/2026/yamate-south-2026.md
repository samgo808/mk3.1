# Yamate South International Residence — 2026

The archived NUCU 2026 guide places CU participants at **Yamate South International Residence, Nagoya University**, during the Nagoya university portion of the trip. It schedules group check-in on **May 18 at 10:00** and checkout on **May 25 at 10:00**. These are 2026 arrangements, not verified 2027 residence policies or bookings (source pages 6, 11, 14–15).

## Location and facilities described by the guide

Address: **21 Yamate-cho, Showa-ku, Nagoya, Aichi 466-0815, Japan**. The guide describes a quiet residential area near Higashiyama Campus, approximately **10 minutes on foot from Nagoya University Station on the Meijo subway line**. Its itinerary separately uses **Yagoto Nisseki Station** for Tsurumai travel; these are different named stations, not alternate spellings.

Rooms are described as single occupancy with a bed, desk, chair, closet, air conditioner, private bathroom and mini-kitchen. **Bedding and kitchenware may be provided or rented, depending on arrangements**; inclusion is not guaranteed. Shared facilities include a laundry room, lounge/common space and security entry system. The guide says Wi-Fi is available in rooms and smoking is not permitted inside the building. Convenience stores, supermarkets, restaurants and public transportation are nearby.

## Arrival and departure rules

General check-in windows in the guide are **09:30–11:00** and **13:00–15:00**. It says missing the window means staff will be unavailable and check-in must wait until the next day. Participants should bring a passport and necessary documents.

The 2026 itinerary has the group leave Best Western Plus at 08:40 on May 18, check in at 10:00 with Samuel and Hasegawa, and set up rooms at 11:00. On May 25 it has **09:50 entrance assembly**, **10:00 checkout**, and **10:30 departure toward Nagoya Station**.

A staff member must inspect rooms at checkout, and the program coordinator must be present. **All participants must check out together; individual checkouts are not permitted** under the guide's arrangement. No individual room assignments or resident records are supplied.

## Supplies and limitations

**Towels, shampoo and laundry detergent are not provided.** The guide recommends bringing a lightweight, quick-drying towel. Laundry facilities require payment; no laundry price is given. Students should distinguish these explicit omissions from bedding/kitchenware, which are conditional.

Public release of these archived group residence arrangements was approved on 2026-09-28. Campus-route images are not recoverable from the supplied text; this page does not infer walking directions from missing maps.
